- Question: Why does God have this conversation with Abraham?
	- God wants to partner with Abraham
	- God is teaching Abraham about his way and the extent of his mercy towards his creation so that Abraham can imitate it.
- Question: Where is the conflict in this story?
	- Abraham sees it as a conflict between him and God, but God doesn't seem to think there is any conflict.
		- Abraham tries to haggle or instruct God in matters of righteousness
		- God intentionally invites Abraham into the conversation
		- God lets Abraham lead the discussion
		- God doesn't object to any of Abraham's requests
		- The conversation ends when Abraham is done
