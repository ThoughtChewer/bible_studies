## Read through [Genesis 2.4-2.25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%202.4-2.25&version=NASB)

- Question: We just read a creation account. Why is there another one that's different?
	- In [[Bible Study 2 - Creation#^creation-ideal|Bible Study 2]] we discussed how [[Genesis 1.1-2.3]] functions as a summary of the bible and the ideal. This leaves the reader waiting to meet the real humans to see what they are like.
	- [[Genesis 1.1-2.3]] was focused on cosmic creation. This one is focused on humans. So here is the story the reader was expecting to get. The author decided that there was something important about the point of creation that was worth partially repeating to communicate.
-  Question: What does the passage say about the source of life?
	- God gives life both directly and indirectly to everything.
		- Direct
			- God breathes life into the form of dust to make it a man.
			- "God caused every tree to grow" in verse 9.
		- Indirect
			- You can see in verse 5 that water from God in combination with man, who is also from God, also results in plant life.
