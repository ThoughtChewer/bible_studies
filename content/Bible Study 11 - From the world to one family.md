## Conclusions:
- If you were not looking for the cycle to repeat, it would have been really easy to miss it. The authors write this way on purpose.
	- Recall [[Psalm 1|Psalm 1.2]], "But his delight is in the Law of the Lord, And on His Law he meditates day and night."
	- In the same way that God has freely given to Abram but Abram is still intimately involved in the process, the authors freely give to us but expect us to be involved at more than a passive level.
