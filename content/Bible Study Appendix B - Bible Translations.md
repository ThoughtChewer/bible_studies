When you translate from one language to another you have to make many choices. The main choice you make is if you are going to translate word-for-word or thought-for-thought. Word-for-word doesn't always capture cultural or implied meanings. Where as, thought-for-thought misses key hyperlink phrases used to navigate the bible. One is not better than the other. They have their tradeoffs, and it is helpful to read multiple translations of the bible as you study it to gain a better overall understanding.

Just to be clear, every translation is an incredible piece of work that requires a high level of expertise and an enormous amount of decisions. I am grateful for all of them, and all of those who did the work to create them. That being said, regardless of which translation you select, there are going to be issues. So learning some Hebrew words and how to use a concordance will be very helpful in understanding what the bible is trying to communicate. Sometimes the English word just doesn't property replicate the idea to the reader. [[Bible Study 3 - Adam and Eve]] and [[Bible Study 5 - Cain and Abel]] have some great examples of how getting into the Hebrew can really enhance your understanding of what is happening that is completely missed in the English translation.
