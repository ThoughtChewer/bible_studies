## Read through: [Genesis 9.18-21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%209.18-21&version=NASB)
- Question: We just had re-creation, but things haven't fundamentally changed. Now we have a story about a garden, fruit, poor choices, and nakedness. What do you think is coming next?
	- Yes, the stories are repeating. The bible doesn't just reference other stories through images. There are structures and cycles linking things together too.
	- For reference [[Genesis 1-11 Structure.png]].
- Even outside of structures in individual stories, the bible uses larger structures to communicate with the reader.
	- Finding them can be difficult. But hopefully you have some tools to look for them now:
		- Specific words (might have to get into the Hebrew)
		- Images (with slightly different words, e.g. wine and fruit, or vineyard and garden)
		- Parallel structures or repeated patterns.
			- You might have noticed that each of the story sections we've covered so far ends with a short poem and a small epilogue. (Might have to read a different translation to have the poem isolated in the text, like the NIV)
