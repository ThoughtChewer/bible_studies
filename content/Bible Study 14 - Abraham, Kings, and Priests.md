## Read through: [Genesis 14.1-14.24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2014.1-14.24&version=NASB)
- Question: Big picture, what is happening in this story?
	- Just let them talk.
- Question: What are interesting things that you noticed?
	- Just let them talk.
- Facilitator: This can be a tricky one to understand what is happening. Start with things that should be familiar and work your way from there. Here is a line of questions that should help identify components of the story. Adjust based on what they notice after reading the story.
	- Question: Do you see any familiar symbols?
		- In verses 18-20, Blessings and wine should be familiar imagery of post flood stories 
			- ref: [[Genesis 6.1-9.17|Genesis 9.1-21]]
			- So you know that the flood comes before that.
	- Question: Who are the chosen and non-chosen brothers?
		- From [[Genesis 13.1-13.18]], We know that Abram is the chosen brother and Lot is not.
	- Question: The chosen brother is saved and the non-chosen brother is "washed away" in the flood. Where is Lot washed away and Abram not?
		- In the battle of the 9 kings, verse 9 if you missed it "four kings against five."
		- So the 4 kings are the flood.
	- Other details they may have noticed:
		- In [[Bible Study 13 - Abram and Lot]] we discussed that Zoar and Sodom are linked to Babylon and are expected to be the recipient of a flood. These are the two cities that bookend the list of 5 kings who lost in the battle.
		- In verse 1, the first king listed is the king of Shinar, aka Babylon as discussed in [[Bible Study 10 - Beginnings of Babylon]], thus linking that story to this one.
		- In [[Bible Study 7 - The Flood]] we discussed that there was a remnant to survive the flood. In verse 13 it says, "Then a survivor came and told Abram..."
- To get some of the deeper meaning of the story we have to do some digging.
	- We have the 4 Mesopotamian kings from Shinar, Ellasar, Elam, and Goiim.
	- The 4 kings defeat several groups of people, verses 5-7, that start with the Rephaim.
		- The [Rephaim](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h7497/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) were a group of people who are giants as well as the others listed.
			- [[Deuteronomy 2]] and [[Deuteronomy 3]] have some more details about them if you want to look.
			- "Superhuman warriors who spread violence in the land  were the heroes of Babylonian and Canaanite culture, but in the biblical vision, they represent everything that's wrong with the human family."[^1]
		- Where was the last place we read about Giants? Right before the flood, in [[Genesis 6.1-9.17|Genesis 6]].
	- Then we have the 5 Canaanite Kings who are also defeated by the 4 kings, and their names give you some insight into who they are:
		- [Bera](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1298/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means son of evil.
			- We were just told in [[Genesis 13.1-13.18|Genesis 13.13]] that the people of Sodom were sinning greatly.
		- [Birsha](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1306/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means with iniquity or wickedness.
		- [Shinab](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8134/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means "splendor of the father" or "father's tooth" or "Sin is my father".
		- [Shemeber](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8038/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means lofty flight.
		- [Bela](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1106/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means destruction or devour.
		- Some of the names are a bit odd, but in general you get the sense that these are not good people.
	- So these 4 kings have come down and destroyed the evil people who live in the land promised to Abram.
- Question: Who survives the flood?
	- The people hanging out with Abram in his "ark", Mamre, Eshcol, and Aner.
	- All 3 of them are Amorites who did not get wiped out with the rest of the Amorites because they joined Abram in God's temple.
	- The blessing of [[Genesis 11.27-12.9|Genesis 12]] are already happening where people on earth are being blessed through Abram.
