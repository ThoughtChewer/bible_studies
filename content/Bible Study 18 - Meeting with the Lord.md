## Read through: [Genesis 18.1-15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2018.1-15&version=NASB)
- Question: Big picture, what is happening in this story?
	- Just let them talk.
- Question: What are interesting things that you noticed?
	- Just let them talk.
- Question: Do you see the imagery?
	- God and his chosen together by sacred trees at the entrance to a tent
	- Taking and giving food
	- Blessings (of children)
	- Woman trusting in her own wisdom (like Eve)
	- Eden = pleasure ("am I to have pleasure")
	- Being afraid
- Question: How has the story changed?
	- Instead of both man and woman participating in the fall its just one of them.
	- The story contrasts the two.
- Question: What is the contrast between Abraham and Sarah?
	- Abraham is generous with God and gives him an offering. 
	- Sarah is still struggling to get beyond her own view of how things work.
	- In [[Genesis 17.1-17.27|Genesis 17.17]], Abraham has a similar response to Sarah in this story, but this time he doesn't respond that way.
		- Irony: [Isaac](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3327/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means "he laughs", and God commanded Abraham to name him that immediately after laughing.
- Question: Where is Abraham?
	- By the Oaks of Mamre. 
	- Question: And where was that last mentioned?
		- In his conflict with Lot
## Conclusions:
- This is a retelling of both the creation story and the rebellion, but you can see through the contrast of the characters that there is a bit of a redemption happening compared to Adam and Eve. Abraham is beginning to trust more in God the more God engages with them.
