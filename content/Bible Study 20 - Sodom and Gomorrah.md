## Conclusions:
- This is the second time that Lot has chosen to run to an evil city instead of choosing to stay in the land with God, just like Cain did in [[Genesis 4.1-4.26]], and it results in tragedy each time as humans continue to provide their own salvation instead of trusting in God's deliverance.
	- Mountain is where God is
	- Zoar was one of the 5 evil cities that was conquered in [[Genesis 13.1-13.18]]
- To Abraham, the decision was for God to destroy the city or spare it to save the righteous. God found a way to both save Lot and cast judgment upon the city. Frequently God will act in ways that we don't think of. So just because it doesn't look like what we expect or want it to be doesn't mean that God isn't acting.
