## Read through: [Genesis 11.27-12.3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2011.27-12.3&version=NASB)
- Question: Big picture, what is happening in this story?
	- Just let them talk.
- Question: What are interesting things that you noticed?
	- Just let them talk.
- Question: Does this sound like a creation story?
	- Maybe?
		- There are humans ruling, but one of the women can't have kids
			- [Sarai](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8297/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means princess in Hebrew.
			- [Milka](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4435/nasb20/wlc/0-1/) means queen in Hebrew.
		- Instead of a story about creation and new life it seems like its a failed adventure that ends in death.
- Chaldeans:
	- Chaldeans = Babylon
	- Babylon tends to invert God's creation (same way it did at the tower of Babel).
- Hebrew authors:
	- If you were not looking for the cycle to repeat, it would have been really easy to miss it. The authors write this way on purpose.
		- Recall [[Psalm 1|Psalm 1.2]], "But his delight is in the Law of the Lord, And on His Law he meditates day and night."
		- In the same way that God has freely given to Abraham but Abraham is still intimately involved in the process, the authors freely give to us but expect us to be involved at more than a passive level.
- Question: What happens that turns the story around and gets us back on track?
	- God speaking. Its the same way that God started to change things in [[Genesis 1.1-2.3|Genesis 1]].
	- This is also the turning point in the story in a structural sense. It goes from God and all of humanity to a focus on one family that God has chosen to bless all of humanity through.
