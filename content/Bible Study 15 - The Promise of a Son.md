- Question: What does this tell you about God?
	- He is caring and will meet people where they are at and address their concerns.
	- He is committed to carrying out his plan though the chosen people.
	- He is willing to work with non-ideal conditions and makes accommodations for them.
		- Covenants instead of faith.
	- He is patient in judgement, and gives people a chance to make a choices.
		- Verse 16, judgement for the Amorites will come in 400 years.
			- These are the group who some got washed up with the giants and some joined Abram in his ark in [[Genesis 14.1-14.24]]. 
		- God allows Abram's descendants to be oppressed for 400 years before judgement comes to those doing it.
	- He exists outside of time, so he knows what people will choose.
